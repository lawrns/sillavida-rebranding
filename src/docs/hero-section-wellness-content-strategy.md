# Hero Section Wellness Content Strategy

## Overview

This document outlines the content strategy for transforming the SillaVida hero section to incorporate wellness-focused messaging that emphasizes the "investing in yourself" theme. The current hero section focuses primarily on product features and gaming-oriented benefits. The redesign calls for a shift toward messaging that highlights how ergonomic chairs contribute to overall wellbeing, productivity, and quality of life.

## Core Message

The core message of the hero section will be "Invierte en tu bienestar, mejora tu productividad" (Invest in your wellbeing, improve your productivity). This message emphasizes the dual benefits of SillaVida chairs: personal wellness and enhanced productivity.

## Target Audience

- Professionals working from home
- Office workers concerned about their physical wellbeing
- Individuals looking to invest in quality furniture that supports their health
- People who value both aesthetics and ergonomics in their workspace

## Content Pillars

1. **Self-Investment**: Positioning ergonomic chairs as an investment in oneself, not just a purchase
2. **Wellness Benefits**: Highlighting specific health benefits of proper seating
3. **Productivity Enhancement**: Connecting physical comfort to improved focus and productivity
4. **Quality of Life**: Emphasizing how proper seating contributes to overall quality of life

## Visual Strategy

- Replace gaming-focused imagery with wellness-focused lifestyle photography
- Show people working comfortably in ergonomic chairs
- Incorporate subtle visual cues that suggest wellbeing (plants, natural light, calm environments)
- Use the "Vida" theme elements to reinforce the wellness focus

## Call-to-Action Strategy

Update the CTA from "Agregar al Carrito" to "Invierte en tu bienestar" to reinforce the self-investment theme.

## Key Benefits to Highlight

1. **Posture Improvement**: Highlight how proper seating supports correct posture
2. **Reduced Pain**: Emphasize reduction in back, neck, and shoulder pain
3. **Increased Energy**: Connect proper ergonomics to reduced fatigue and increased energy
4. **Enhanced Focus**: Link physical comfort to improved concentration and focus
5. **Long-term Health**: Position ergonomic chairs as a preventative health measure

## Tone and Voice

- Warm and supportive
- Professional but not clinical
- Empowering and positive
- Focused on benefits rather than features
- Emphasizing value rather than price

## Implementation Notes

- Maintain the slider functionality while updating content
- Ensure the messaging is effective across all device sizes
