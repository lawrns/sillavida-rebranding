# Historias de Vida: Testimonial Collection Framework

## Overview
This document outlines the framework for collecting, curating, and presenting customer testimonials for the SillaVida "Historias de Vida" carousel. Unlike standard product reviews, these testimonials focus on how ergonomic chairs have improved customers' quality of life, health, and productivity, reinforcing the "investing in yourself" theme and "Vida" concept.

## Testimonial Categories
Testimonials are categorized by primary benefit type to help users find relevant stories:

1. **Health Improvement**
   - Reduced back/neck pain
   - Better posture
   - Decreased fatigue
   - Improved circulation
   - Reduced stress on joints

2. **Productivity Enhancement**
   - Increased focus and concentration
   - Extended comfortable working periods
   - Fewer breaks needed due to discomfort
   - Better energy levels throughout the day
   - Enhanced work quality

3. **Comfort & Wellbeing**
   - Overall comfort experience
   - Improved mood and satisfaction
   - Better work-life balance
   - Enhanced home office experience
   - General quality of life improvements

## Testimonial Structure
Each testimonial should include:

1. **Customer Information**
   - Name (first name and last initial, or full name with permission)
   - Age range (optional)
   - Profession/role
   - Location (city/region)
   - Photo or avatar (with permission)

2. **Context**
   - Previous situation/pain points
   - Type of work/activities
   - Duration of issues before solution
   - Previous solutions attempted

3. **Transformation Story**
   - Chair model purchased
   - Length of time using the chair
   - Specific improvements experienced
   - Quantifiable benefits where possible (e.g., "50% reduction in back pain")
   - Emotional impact of the change

4. **Conclusion**
   - Overall satisfaction
   - Would they recommend to others
   - Any additional unexpected benefits

## Collection Methods

### 1. Post-Purchase Follow-up
- Email customers 30-60 days after purchase
- Include specific questions about life improvements
- Offer incentive for detailed testimonials (discount on accessories, etc.)

### 2. Social Media Monitoring
- Monitor brand mentions and hashtags
- Reach out to customers sharing positive experiences
- Request permission to feature their stories

### 3. Customer Support Interactions
- Train support team to identify potential testimonial candidates
- Flag accounts with positive feedback for follow-up
- Create a simple referral process

### 4. Website Submission Form
- Create dedicated "Share Your Story" page
- Include guided questions based on testimonial structure
- Allow photo uploads and category selection

## Curation Guidelines

### Selection Criteria
1. **Authenticity** - Stories should be genuine and verifiable
2. **Specificity** - Preference for detailed accounts over generic praise
3. **Transformation** - Clear before/after narrative
4. **Relevance** - Aligns with "Vida" concept and wellness focus
5. **Diversity** - Variety of demographics, use cases, and chair models

### Editing Guidelines
1. Maintain the customer's voice and perspective
2. Edit only for clarity, grammar, and length
3. Highlight specific benefits and transformative aspects
4. Ensure all claims are reasonable and not medically exaggerated
5. Obtain approval from customer on final edited version

## Display Considerations

### Presentation Elements
1. **Visual** - Customer photo/avatar with consistent styling
2. **Category Badge** - Icon indicating primary benefit category
3. **Quote Highlight** - Key quote or benefit statement
4. **Chair Model** - Reference to specific product used
5. **Verification Mark** - Indicator of verified purchase/customer

### Rotation Strategy
1. Ensure representation across all benefit categories
2. Feature newer testimonials more frequently
3. Prioritize testimonials relevant to viewed product categories
4. Include mix of demographic profiles
5. Adjust based on engagement metrics

## Implementation Timeline
1. Create collection mechanisms (email templates, forms) - Week 1
2. Begin collecting initial testimonials - Weeks 2-4
3. Develop curation process and edit initial submissions - Week 5
4. Implement carousel component with placeholder content - Weeks 3-4
5. Replace placeholders with real testimonials - Week 6
6. Monitor engagement and refine - Ongoing

## Placeholder Content
Until sufficient real testimonials are collected, develop placeholder content that:
1. Represents realistic scenarios based on customer feedback
2. Clearly indicates it's a representation (not misleading)
3. Covers all benefit categories
4. Aligns with actual product benefits
5. Can be gradually replaced with real testimonials

## Legal Considerations
1. Obtain explicit permission to use customer stories
2. Create clear terms for testimonial submissions
3. Avoid medical claims or guarantees
4. Maintain documentation of permissions
5. Provide customers ability to remove their testimonial if requested
