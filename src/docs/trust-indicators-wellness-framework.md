# Trust Indicators Wellness Framework

## Overview

This document outlines the framework for transforming SillaVida's trust indicators to align with the "investing in yourself" theme and "Vida" concept. Traditional e-commerce trust elements (warranty, shipping, payment options) will be reframed to emphasize how they support the customer's investment in their wellbeing, creating a cohesive narrative throughout the purchase journey.

## Core Message

**"Tu bienestar, nuestra prioridad"** (Your wellbeing, our priority)

This core message emphasizes that SillaVida's commitment to quality, service, and customer satisfaction is fundamentally about supporting the customer's wellbeing. It shifts the focus from transactional trust (protecting a purchase) to wellness trust (protecting an investment in oneself).

## Trust Categories

### 1. Garantía de Bienestar (Wellbeing Guarantee)

**Traditional Concept:** Warranty protection
**Wellness Reframe:** A commitment to protecting the customer's investment in their health and comfort

**Key Messages:**
- "Respaldamos tu inversión en bienestar" (We back your investment in wellbeing)
- "Garantía de 5 años para tu tranquilidad" (5-year guarantee for your peace of mind)
- "Compromiso con tu salud a largo plazo" (Commitment to your long-term health)

**Visual Elements:**
- Shield icon with a subtle leaf or wellness symbol integrated
- Soft, reassuring colors from the SillaVida palette (sage, teal)
- Typography that emphasizes stability and trust

### 2. Envío Consciente (Mindful Shipping)

**Traditional Concept:** Shipping options and policies
**Wellness Reframe:** A thoughtful delivery process designed to bring wellness to your space with minimal stress

**Key Messages:**
- "Tu camino hacia el bienestar comienza ahora" (Your path to wellbeing begins now)
- "Entrega cuidadosa, instalación sin estrés" (Careful delivery, stress-free setup)
- "Seguimiento transparente para tu tranquilidad" (Transparent tracking for your peace of mind)

**Visual Elements:**
- Flowing path or journey icon with leaf elements
- Calming colors that suggest movement and progress
- Typography that conveys reliability with a touch of warmth

### 3. Pago Sereno (Serene Payment)

**Traditional Concept:** Secure payment options
**Wellness Reframe:** A peaceful, stress-free investment process

**Key Messages:**
- "Invierte en ti mismo con total tranquilidad" (Invest in yourself with complete peace of mind)
- "Proceso de pago diseñado para tu serenidad" (Payment process designed for your serenity)
- "Opciones flexibles que se adaptan a tu vida" (Flexible options that adapt to your life)

**Visual Elements:**
- Lock icon with subtle wellness elements (perhaps a heart or leaf integrated)
- Calming colors that suggest security and peace
- Typography that balances professionalism with approachability

### 4. Compromiso Vida (Life Commitment)

**Traditional Concept:** Company values and customer service
**Wellness Reframe:** A holistic commitment to supporting the customer's quality of life

**Key Messages:**
- "Más que una compra, una inversión en tu calidad de vida" (More than a purchase, an investment in your quality of life)
- "Nuestro compromiso: tu bienestar duradero" (Our commitment: your lasting wellbeing)
- "Soporte humano que entiende tus necesidades" (Human support that understands your needs)

**Visual Elements:**
- Heart or handshake icon with "Vida" elements integrated
- Warm, inviting colors that suggest care and commitment
- Typography that feels personal and genuine

## Implementation Guidelines

### Visual Badge System

Each trust category will have a corresponding visual badge with:

1. **Icon:** A recognizable trust symbol (shield, lock, etc.) with subtle wellness elements integrated
2. **Color:** Using the SillaVida color palette, with emphasis on:
   - Sage: For nurturing, growth, and wellbeing
   - Teal: For trust, stability, and professionalism
   - Warm accents: For human connection and care
3. **Shape:** Organic, slightly rounded shapes that align with the "Vida" theme
4. **Typography:** Using the SillaVida typography system, with:
   - Headings: Font-heading with medium weight for trust category names
   - Body: Font-body for descriptions, with slightly increased line height for readability

### Placement Strategy

#### Product Pages
- Prominent "Garantía de Bienestar" badge near the price and add-to-cart button
- "Compromiso Vida" section in the product details, highlighting the wellness benefits
- Shipping and payment trust indicators in a clean, unobtrusive row

#### Cart Page
- "Pago Sereno" messaging prominently displayed near checkout button
- "Envío Consciente" information with estimated delivery dates
- Subtle reminder of the "Garantía de Bienestar"

#### Checkout Page
- Reinforcement of all trust elements in a sidebar
- "Compromiso Vida" statement at the completion of checkout
- Final reassurance messaging that emphasizes the wellness investment

### Tone and Voice

- **Warm:** Convey genuine care for the customer's wellbeing
- **Reassuring:** Build confidence without being overly formal
- **Mindful:** Use language that acknowledges the importance of the customer's investment in themselves
- **Clear:** Maintain clarity about policies while using wellness-focused language
- **Authentic:** Avoid excessive promises; focus on genuine commitments to customer wellbeing

### Language Patterns

- Use "inversión" (investment) rather than "compra" (purchase)
- Emphasize "bienestar" (wellbeing) and "calidad de vida" (quality of life)
- Connect features to peace of mind and reduced stress
- Use "respaldamos" (we support/back) rather than just "garantizamos" (we guarantee)
- Incorporate "Vida" terminology throughout trust messaging

## Specific Applications

### Warranty Page Transformation

**Current Heading:** "Garantía y Devoluciones"
**New Heading:** "Garantía de Bienestar"

**Current Intro:** "Nuestros productos están respaldados por una garantía de 5 años que cubre defectos de fabricación."
**New Intro:** "Tu inversión en bienestar está respaldada por nuestra Garantía de Bienestar de 5 años, diseñada para darte la tranquilidad de que tu camino hacia una mejor calidad de vida está protegido."

### Shipping Information Transformation

**New Heading:** "Envío Consciente"

**New Intro:** "Tu camino hacia el bienestar comienza con nuestro servicio de Envío Consciente, diseñado para traer confort y salud a tu espacio en 3-5 días hábiles, con actualizaciones transparentes en cada paso."

## Conclusion

This framework transforms standard e-commerce trust indicators into wellness-focused elements that reinforce the "investing in yourself" theme and "Vida" concept. By reframing warranties, shipping, payments, and customer service in terms of wellbeing, peace of mind, and quality of life, we create a cohesive narrative that supports the customer throughout their journey with SillaVida.

The implementation of this framework will help build deeper trust with customers by demonstrating that SillaVida's commitment goes beyond the transaction to genuinely supporting their investment in their own wellbeing.
