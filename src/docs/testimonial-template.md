# Historias de Vida: Testimonial Template

## Overview
This document provides a standardized template for "Historias de Vida" testimonials, ensuring consistency in structure while allowing for authentic customer voices. Each testimonial should focus on the transformative impact of ergonomic chairs on customers' overall wellbeing and daily life, reinforcing the "investing in yourself" theme and "Vida" concept.

## Adaptation Guidelines

1. **Maintain Authenticity**: While following the structure, preserve the customer's unique voice and perspective.

2. **Focus on Transformation**: Emphasize the before/after contrast to highlight the impact on quality of life.

3. **Be Specific**: Include concrete details about improvements rather than generic statements.

4. **Highlight "Vida" Concept**: Reinforce how the chair has enhanced overall life quality, not just work comfort.

5. **Keep it Concise**: Each section should be brief while conveying the essential information.

6. **Avoid Exaggeration**: Ensure all claims are reasonable and credible.

7. **Include Emotional Impact**: Address both physical benefits and emotional/psychological improvements.

## Usage Instructions

1. Use this template when collecting testimonials through all channels (email, forms, interviews).

2. Provide this structure to customers as a guide, not a rigid format they must follow.

3. When editing customer submissions, maintain their key points and unique perspective while organizing according to this template.

4. Ensure all testimonials receive final approval from the customer before publication.

5. Tag each testimonial with the appropriate category for filtering and display purposes.
