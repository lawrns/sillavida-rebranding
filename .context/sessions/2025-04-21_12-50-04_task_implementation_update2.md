Continuing implementation of TASK-046: Create "Por qué invertir en una silla ergonómica" Educational Section.

After creating the framework document and gathering statistics and research, we've now developed the detailed content for the educational section. This content transforms the technical information and statistics into engaging, persuasive content that effectively communicates the value of investing in an ergonomic chair.

- Created a comprehensive content document (`src/docs/ergonomic-chair-educational-content.md`) that includes:
  - An introduction that frames ergonomic chairs as an investment in wellbeing
  - Detailed sections on health benefits, productivity improvements, and long-term value
  - Information on key ergonomic features and their specific benefits
  - A conclusion that reinforces the "investing in yourself" theme
  - Integration of statistics and expert quotes from the research document
  - Content that balances educational value with persuasive elements

- Organized the content to follow the three-part structure established in the framework document
- Used a conversational, second-person tone to engage the reader directly
- Included "¿Sabías que?" (Did you know?) sections to highlight key statistics in an engaging way
- Incorporated expert quotes to add credibility and authority to the content
- Used bullet points for better readability and to break up dense information
- Emphasized the "investing in yourself" theme throughout the content
- Maintained a balance between technical information and relatable benefits
