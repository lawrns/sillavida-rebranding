## Decisions
1. **Life-Aspect Categories Structure**
   - Six primary life-aspect categories: Vida Profesional, Vida Activa, Vida Saludable, Vida Productiva, Vida Social, and Complementos para tu Vida
   - Secondary navigation elements: Promociones, Más Vendidos, Ergonomía
   - "More" dropdown for additional life aspects on smaller screens

2. **URL Structure**
   - `/vida/[life-aspect]` pattern instead of `/category/[product-type]`
   - 301 redirects from old category URLs to new life-aspect URLs
   - Canonical tags to indicate preferred URLs for search engines

3. **Visual Design Approach**
   - Distinct colors from the SillaVida color palette for each life aspect
   - Unique icons that visually represent each life-aspect concept
   - Subtle animations to enhance the navigation experience
