# Decision to Put TASK-049 on Hold

## Context
TASK-049 "Implement Benefit-Focused Product Page Tabs" was recently activated to redesign the product page tabs to focus on benefits rather than features. Initial design work has been completed, including a comprehensive specification document, implementation planning, and design decisions documentation. However, the user has requested to put this task on hold and move on to another task.

## Decision
We have decided to:

1. Put TASK-049 "Implement Benefit-Focused Product Page Tabs" on hold
2. Preserve the design work completed so far for future implementation
3. Move on to another task as requested by the user

This decision was made based on the user's direct request to prioritize other tasks over TASK-049 at this time.

## Rationale
1. **User Direction**: The user explicitly requested to put TASK-049 on hold and move on to another task, indicating a change in priorities.

2. **Resource Allocation**: By focusing on one task at a time, we can ensure that resources are allocated efficiently and work is completed to a high standard.

3. **Project Flow**: Task prioritization is a normal part of project management and allows for flexibility in responding to changing requirements or priorities.

4. **Preservation of Work**: The design work completed for TASK-049 (specification document, implementation planning, and design decisions) is preserved and can be used when the task is reactivated, ensuring no effort is wasted.

## Alternatives Considered
1. **Continue with TASK-049**: We could have continued with the implementation of the benefit-focused tabs. However, this would not align with the user's current priorities.

2. **Work on Multiple Tasks Simultaneously**: We could have attempted to work on TASK-049 alongside other tasks. However, this would likely result in divided attention and potentially lower quality outcomes for all tasks.

3. **Cancel TASK-049**: We could have cancelled TASK-049 entirely. However, putting it on hold instead preserves the design work completed and allows for it to be resumed in the future.

## Impact
- **Timeline**: The implementation of the benefit-focused tabs will be delayed until TASK-049 is reactivated.
- **Resources**: Resources that would have been allocated to TASK-049 can now be fully dedicated to other tasks.
- **Project Scope**: The overall project scope remains unchanged, as TASK-049 is only on hold, not cancelled.
- **Documentation**: Comprehensive documentation of the design work completed for TASK-049 ensures that work can be resumed efficiently in the future.
