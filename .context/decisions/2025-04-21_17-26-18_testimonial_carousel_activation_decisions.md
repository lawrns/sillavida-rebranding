# Testimonial Carousel Activation Decisions

## Context
TASK-047 "Create 'Historias de Vida' Testimonial Carousel" has been activated to implement a new testimonial section for the SillaVida website. This component will showcase real customer stories about how ergonomic chairs have improved their quality of life, health, and productivity, reinforcing the "investing in yourself" theme and "Vida" concept.

## Decisions

### 1. Activation Timing
**Decision**: Activate TASK-047 now that all dependencies are completed.
**Rationale**: The prerequisite tasks (TASK-040, TASK-041, TASK-042) related to color palette, typography, and "Vida" theme integration have been completed, providing the necessary design foundation for the testimonial carousel.

### 2. Implementation Approach
**Decision**: Begin with a framework for collecting and curating testimonials before implementing the UI component.
**Rationale**: Establishing a clear framework for testimonial content will ensure consistency and quality in the stories presented, making the UI implementation more focused and effective.

### 3. Content Focus
**Decision**: Focus testimonials on life improvements rather than product features.
**Rationale**: This approach aligns with the "Vida" concept and emphasizes the transformative impact of investing in ergonomic seating on customers' overall wellbeing and daily life, creating a more compelling narrative.

### 4. Categorization Strategy
**Decision**: Categorize testimonials by benefit type (health improvement, productivity, comfort).
**Rationale**: This organization will help users find relevant stories that address their specific concerns or interests, making the testimonials more impactful and relatable.

### 5. Visual Design Direction
**Decision**: Use the new color palette and typography established in previous tasks, incorporating "Vida" theme elements.
**Rationale**: Maintaining visual consistency with the rest of the site while emphasizing the "Vida" theme will create a cohesive user experience and reinforce the brand message.

### 6. Implementation Sequence
**Decision**: Follow the step-by-step approach outlined in the task description, starting with content framework and template development.
**Rationale**: This structured approach ensures that the foundation is solid before moving to design and implementation, reducing the need for rework and ensuring a high-quality final product.

## Impact Analysis

### User Experience
- Enhanced social proof through authentic customer stories
- Stronger emotional connection to the brand and products
- Better understanding of the real-life benefits of ergonomic chairs

### Business Goals
- Reinforcement of the "investing in yourself" value proposition
- Potential increase in conversion rates through compelling social proof
- Differentiation from competitors who focus primarily on product features

### Technical Considerations
- Need for responsive and accessible carousel implementation
- Integration with existing site architecture and Shopify backend
- Performance optimization for all devices
