# Enhance Trust Indicators with Wellness-Focused Messaging

## Description
This task involves transforming the trust indicators across the SillaVida website to align with the "investing in yourself" theme and "Vida" concept. The current trust indicators (warranty, shipping, payment options) use standard e-commerce messaging without connecting to the wellness and self-investment themes. The redesign calls for reframing these trust elements to emphasize how they support the customer's investment in their wellbeing, creating a cohesive narrative throughout the purchase journey.

## Objectives
- Reframe warranty messaging as "Garantía de bienestar" (Wellbeing Guarantee)
- Create visual badges for quality standards that emphasize health benefits
- Update shipping and payment messaging to emphasize peace of mind
- Develop consistent trust messaging for product pages, cart, and checkout
- Create a "Compromiso Vida" (Life Commitment) section highlighting the brand's dedication to customer wellbeing
- Ensure all trust indicators visually align with the new color palette and typography
- Incorporate subtle "Vida" theme elements in trust indicator designs
- Maintain clarity and effectiveness of trust messaging while adding the wellness focus

## Steps
1. Create a trust messaging framework document that outlines the wellness-focused approach
2. Design visual badges for key trust indicators (warranty, quality, shipping, payment)
3. Rewrite warranty messaging to focus on "Garantía de bienestar"
4. Update shipping and payment messaging to emphasize peace of mind
5. Create copy for a "Compromiso Vida" section
6. Identify all components that display trust indicators (product pages, cart, checkout)
7. Update the trust indicator components with the new messaging and visual elements
8. Ensure all trust indicators use the new color palette and typography
9. Add subtle "Vida" theme elements to trust indicator designs
10. Test the updated trust indicators for clarity and effectiveness
11. Verify that the messaging effectively communicates the "investing in yourself" theme
12. Ensure the trust indicators are responsive and work well on mobile devices
