# Update Hero Section with Wellness-Focused Messaging

## Description
This task involves transforming the hero section of the SillaVida website to incorporate wellness-focused messaging that emphasizes the "investing in yourself" theme. The current hero section focuses primarily on product features and gaming-oriented benefits. The redesign calls for a shift toward messaging that highlights how ergonomic chairs contribute to overall wellbeing, productivity, and quality of life. This messaging overhaul will be a key component in repositioning SillaVida as a wellness-focused brand.

## Objectives
- Update the hero section headline to "Invierte en tu bienestar, mejora tu productividad"
- Replace gaming-focused imagery with wellness-focused lifestyle photography
- Create new subheadlines that emphasize health benefits and self-investment
- Implement a new value proposition focused on long-term wellbeing
- Ensure the hero section visually aligns with the new color palette and typography
- Incorporate subtle "Vida" theme elements in the hero section design
- Maintain the functionality of the hero slider while updating its content
- Ensure the messaging is effective across all device sizes

## Steps
1. Create new copy for the hero section headline and subheadlines
2. Develop a content strategy document for the hero section that outlines the wellness-focused messaging
3. Identify and source appropriate wellness-focused lifestyle photography
4. Update the HeroSlider.tsx component to use the new headline and subheadlines
5. Replace the current hero images with new wellness-focused imagery
6. Adjust the layout and spacing to accommodate the new content
7. Ensure the hero section uses the new color palette and typography
8. Add subtle "Vida" theme elements to the hero section design
9. Update the CTA text and styling to align with the wellness focus
10. Ensure the hero section is responsive and works well on mobile devices
11. Test the updated hero section across different browsers and devices
12. Verify that the messaging effectively communicates the "investing in yourself" theme
