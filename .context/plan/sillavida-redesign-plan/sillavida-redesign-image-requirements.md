# SillaVida Redesign Image Requirements Documentation

## Overview

This document provides detailed specifications for the imagery required to implement the SillaVida website redesign. High-quality, consistent imagery is essential for effectively communicating the "investing in yourself" theme and "Vida" concept. The images will play a crucial role in shifting the brand positioning from feature-focused to benefit-focused, highlighting how ergonomic chairs contribute to overall wellbeing, productivity, and quality of life.

## Image Categories

### 1. Lifestyle Photography

#### Hero Section Images

**Purpose**: To create an immediate emotional connection with the wellness-focused brand positioning

**Requirements**:
- 3-5 high-quality hero images showing people experiencing improved comfort and productivity
- Resolution: 1920px × 800px minimum (2560px × 1080px recommended for retina displays)
- Format: WebP with JPEG fallback
- Aspect ratio: 2.4:1 (desktop) with mobile-friendly crop options
- Style: Natural lighting, warm tones, authentic rather than overly staged

**Specific Shots Needed**:
1. Professional working comfortably in an ergonomic chair, showing good posture and relaxed concentration
2. Person transitioning between sitting and standing, demonstrating flexibility and movement
3. Home office setup with ergonomic chair as the centerpiece of a wellness-focused workspace
4. Close-up of person with relaxed shoulders and proper posture, visibly comfortable
5. Wide shot of collaborative workspace with multiple people using ergonomic chairs

#### Workspace Context Images

**Purpose**: To show chairs in real-life contexts that resonate with the target audience

**Requirements**:
- 8-12 context images showing chairs in various environments
- Resolution: 1200px × 800px minimum
- Format: WebP with JPEG fallback
- Aspect ratio: 3:2
- Style: Consistent with hero images, showing authentic workspaces

**Specific Shots Needed**:
1. Home office setup with natural light and plants (Vida Saludable theme)
2. Professional office environment with ergonomic workstation (Vida Profesional theme)
3. Creative workspace with collaborative elements (Vida Creativa theme)
4. Gaming setup with proper ergonomic positioning (Vida Activa theme)
5. Minimalist, wellness-focused workspace with meditation elements
6. Family-friendly workspace showing work-life balance
7. Standing desk with ergonomic chair nearby, showing flexibility
8. Nighttime workspace with proper lighting and ergonomic setup

#### People-Focused Wellness Images

**Purpose**: To illustrate the health and wellness benefits of ergonomic seating

**Requirements**:
- 6-8 images showing people experiencing improved wellbeing
- Resolution: 800px × 1200px minimum
- Format: WebP with JPEG fallback
- Aspect ratio: 2:3 (portrait orientation)
- Style: Authentic, diverse representation, focus on positive expressions

**Specific Shots Needed**:

## Image Style Guide

### Color Treatment

All images should align with the new color palette:
- Deep teal (#1E5959) as the primary color
- Warm beige (#E8DED1) for backgrounds and secondary elements
- Sage green (#7D9D8C) for accent elements
- Muted terracotta (#C87D55) as a highlight color for key features
- Black (#212529) for text elements within images

### Lighting

- Natural, soft lighting that creates a warm, inviting atmosphere
- Avoid harsh shadows or overly dramatic lighting
- Consistent light direction across related images
- Soft window light preferred for lifestyle shots
- Directional lighting to highlight specific features in product shots

### Composition

- Rule of thirds for balanced, engaging compositions
- Clear focal points that draw attention to key elements
- Adequate negative space to maintain a clean, uncluttered look
- Consistent horizon lines and perspectives across related images
- Thoughtful framing that emphasizes the subject

### Mood and Tone

- Calm, focused, and productive atmosphere
- Warm, inviting, and approachable feeling
- Natural and authentic rather than overly polished
- Wellness-oriented without being clinical
- Professional without being corporate or sterile

### People and Diversity

- Diverse representation across age, gender, ethnicity, and body types
- Authentic expressions and natural poses
- Professional but approachable styling
- Inclusive representation of different work environments and styles
- Focus on genuine comfort and wellbeing rather than staged poses

## Technical Specifications

### File Formats and Optimization

- Primary format: WebP for optimal compression and quality
- Fallback format: JPEG for photos, PNG for graphics with transparency
- SVG for all icons, badges, and infographics when possible
- Progressive loading for larger images
- Appropriate compression to balance quality and performance:
  - Hero images: 80-85% quality
  - Product images: 85-90% quality
  - Thumbnails: 75-80% quality

### Responsive Image Requirements

- Multiple sizes for each image to support responsive design:
  - Desktop (1x and 2x)
  - Tablet (1x and 2x)
  - Mobile (1x and 2x)
- Appropriate cropping for different aspect ratios
- Art direction for images that need different compositions at different sizes
- Proper implementation of srcset and sizes attributes

### Accessibility Requirements

- All images must have appropriate alt text
- Decorative images should have empty alt attributes
- Complex infographics need detailed descriptions
- Sufficient contrast for text overlays on images (4.5:1 minimum)
- No critical information conveyed through color alone
- Avoid text as part of images when possible

## Implementation Guidelines

### Image Naming Convention

Use the following naming convention for all images:
- `sillavida-[category]-[subject]-[variant]-[size].[format]`

Examples:
- `sillavida-hero-office-main-desktop.webp`
- `sillavida-product-ergopro-lumbar-square.webp`
- `sillavida-icon-posture-primary.svg`

### Image Organization

Organize images in the following directory structure:
```
/images
  /hero
  /lifestyle
  /products
    /ergopro
    /xgamer
    /etc
  /icons
  /patterns
  /infographics
  /badges
```

### Lazy Loading Implementation

- Implement lazy loading for all images below the fold
- Use native loading="lazy" attribute where supported
- Implement a JavaScript fallback for browsers without native support
- Consider using intersection observer for more control
- Implement LQIP (Low Quality Image Placeholders) for larger images

### Image CDN Recommendations

- Use a CDN with image optimization capabilities
- Implement automatic WebP conversion with fallbacks
- Enable automatic responsive image generation
- Implement caching with appropriate cache headers
- Consider using Cloudinary, Imgix, or similar service

## Image Acquisition Strategy

### Custom Photography

For lifestyle and product photography, we recommend:
- Professional photoshoot with experienced product and lifestyle photographer
- Detailed shot list based on this document
- Proper styling and art direction to maintain consistency
- Professional models representing diverse demographics
- Controlled environment for product photography
- Natural environment for lifestyle photography

### Stock Photography Adaptation

If budget constraints require stock photography:
- Select images that can be edited to match the color palette
- Choose consistent style across all selected images
- Avoid obviously staged or corporate stock photos
- Edit all stock photos to maintain consistent color treatment
- Supplement with custom photography for key hero images

### Graphic Element Creation

For icons, patterns, and infographics:
- Work with a graphic designer experienced in brand identity
- Develop a consistent style guide for all graphic elements
- Create all elements as scalable vector graphics
- Ensure all elements work in both light and dark modes
- Maintain consistency with the "Vida" theme

## Budget Considerations

### Recommended Allocation

- Professional Photography: 50-60% of image budget
- Graphic Design Elements: 20-30% of image budget
- Stock Photography (if needed): 10-15% of image budget
- Image Optimization and CDN: 10-15% of image budget

### Cost-Saving Options

- Phased photography approach, starting with key products
- Hybrid approach using limited custom photography with stock photos
- Template-based infographics with custom content
- Prioritize SVG development for scalability and reduced need for multiple sizes
